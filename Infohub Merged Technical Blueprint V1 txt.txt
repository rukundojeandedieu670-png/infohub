InfoHub - Technical & Product Blueprint
Vision
InfoHub is designed to become a centralized digital platform for information, opportunities, and business visibility in Rwanda.

The platform combines:

• News and public information
• Job opportunities
• Business listings
• Educational resources
• Events and announcements
     • Community engagement The system should feel:
• Reliable
• Professional
• Fast
• Mobile-friendly
• Trustworthy
• Opportunity-driven


1. PLATFORM OBJECTIVES
Primary Goals

• Deliver organized and searchable information
• Encourage repeat visitors
• Support businesses and employers
• Build a trusted digital community
• Create sustainable monetization channels
• Prepare for future mobile applications
